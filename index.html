Straight answers

The questions you're already asking.

Is it really free?

Yes. No fee, no deposit. The only money that ever moves is the Kindness Pledge fine if you miss a milestone, and all of it goes to Sathkaara.

I'm over 60. Is this really for me?

Yes. Retirees bring savings, patience and networks that twenty-somethings would kill for. Your circle will need you as much as you need it.

Do I have to take a loan?

No. Finishing Launchpad pre-qualifies you for Future Life business funding if you want it. Approval still follows FLI's normal credit check. Nothing is conditional on borrowing, and no mentor is measured on loans.

What does Future Life actually get?

Entrepreneurs who've proven they can execute. Some become customers, some borrow, some supply our shelves and hotels. All of them make the Future Life name mean something in their town. We'd rather say that than have you guess it.

Where and when?

Circles meet at CLIO properties in the intake city, with daily tasks on your phone in between. Sinhala first, with Tamil circles in the second intake.
